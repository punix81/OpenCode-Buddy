# Enclosure

Status: deferred to Phase 6.

No mechanical design exists yet and no measurement has been taken. Constraints known today:

- Round 1.28" display with a 240 px visible diameter; the module's bezel is larger than the glass.
- ESP32-C3 Super Mini is small but the USB-C connector must remain accessible for power and data.
- The TTP223 pad must be reachable through the shell (thin wall or a dedicated window).
- No ventilation requirement is known; no thermal measurement has been taken.

Phase 6 will add: dimensions, a printable model (STEP + STL), print settings, assembly notes and a
photo of the assembled unit. Any model must keep the USB connector usable without disassembly.

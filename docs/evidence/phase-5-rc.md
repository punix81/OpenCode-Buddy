## 6. Hardware gates (not met)

- 20 genuine cold boots and 10 USB reconnections were skipped in the 4.1 acceptance; they remain
  required for the RC.
- The first handshake after a long no-host uptime failed once in the earlier Phase 5 session; the
  diagnostic LIVE image is prepared to isolate it. The RC must not be accepted before that
  investigation and a new cold-boot series.
- The 3600 s soak must be re-run on this bridge (the 4.1 soak does not count) and the 8 h endurance
  remains.
- The RC is not flashed; no claim of hardware timing, panel pixels or handshake reliability.

## 7. Flash and rollback (when authorised)

1. Stop the bridge; verify the device (`303a:1001`, MAC `44:BD:8D:4E:27:A4`).
2. The verified application read-back of the installed image is archived at
   `.backups/phase4-2026-11-26/firmware-phase4-1-release-2ff67b71.bin` (`2ff67b71…`, 411 232 B);
   verify the hash before relying on it. The older `firmware-phase4-release.bin` is the Phase 4

3. Write the RC application-only at `0x10000` with esptool 5.7.0 `--no-stub`, then verify by an
   independent read-back and SHA-256.
4. Start the bridge, validate the handshake and the six screens; run the cold-boot series and the
   soak. Rollback = flash `2ff67b71…` at `0x10000` with the same procedure.


## 8. Diagnostic campaign (2026-11-27)

- 4.1 baseline before any flash: 60 s / 300 s / 900 s **3/3 PASS**, 0 retry, 0 invalid, 0 reconnect.
- Authorised diagnostic `3646d4ce…`: flash verified; first opening failed (ready, 0 ACK, 3 retries)
  with `rx_bytes=16`, `rx_lines=0`, `dropped=26`, `hello=24`, `hb=24`.
- Release 4.1 after rollback: first two sessions failed, then recovered after idle; a real power
  cycle (device 22) restored the handshake.
- Instrumentation defects (direct printf, no-flush diagnostic TxBuffer) were found and fixed in
  diagnostic-only builds; they explain part of the earlier severity but not the release failure.
- `fix3` (`3689b828…`): `hb=0` but 26 pre-link hellos (`hostConnected` is enumeration-based),
  `rx_bytes=16`, no ACK.
- `fix4` (`0b57ca8c…`): host-byte hello gate; `hello=0`, `rx_bytes=0`, bridge “no hello received” —
  the gate deadlocks when the RX path is dead.
- Root cause of the RX stall is **not proven** (USB peripheral vs HWCDC ring vs host CDC state).
  Workaround: real power cycle/replug; no validated firmware fix. The experimental USB policy was
  reverted (`d564f48`); the release source reproduces RC1 code (fresh build `f9cc72ee…`,
  code/data-identical to `dd7d1d57…`).
- The final RC is not built/flashed. The cold-boot, reconnect, soak and endurance gates remain open.
  Details: `docs/evidence/phase-5-usb-diagnostics.md`.
